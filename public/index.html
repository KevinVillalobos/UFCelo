▶ How it works

Multi-factor ELO

One ELO per fighter, whatever the division. Every fight moves points according to how surprising the result was, adjusted by 12 multipliers: method and round, streak, opponent, rematch, fight time and more.

7D skill score

Striking, Defense, Grappling, Consistency, Finish Rate, Cardio/Durability and Pressure, each 0 to 100, built from per-fight statistics and smoothed with an exponential moving average.

Monte Carlo

We simulate the fight 100 to 10,000 times to estimate the probability of each outcome, the most likely method and the round it ends in.

Validation

We measure the engine against real results using the probability computed before each fight. See the historical accuracy block for how often it is right, and its limits.

Technical details

Win probability. P(A) = 1 / (1 + 10(ELOB − ELOA)/400). A 200-point gap gives ~76%; 400 gives ~91%. The gap is capped at ±250 so the model never claims certainty.

Variable K. New fighters (<5 fights) move more points; with 15+ fights the rating is steadier. Each division has a multiplier (Heavyweight 1.0×, Welterweight 0.75×).

Method and round. KO/TKO in R1: 1.40×, R2: 1.25×, R3+: 1.10×. Submission: 1.20–1.25×. Unanimous decision: 1.05×, majority: 0.90×, split: 0.80×. Title fights: +1.20×.

Streaks. A 3-win streak adds 1.15× and a 3-loss streak adds 1.25× to the loser's drop.

Inactivity. An inactive fighter slowly drifts back toward 1500, so retired fighters do not hold top spots.

Final prediction. ELO probability ± an adjustment of up to ±10% from the composite skill difference.
